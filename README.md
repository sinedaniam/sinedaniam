# Denis Mburu

**Economist · Statistician · Data Scientist** · Nairobi, Kenya

I turn complex data into clear, evidence-based insights that inform policy and public investment.
I currently work at the **Kenya National Highways Authority**, where my work combines economics, infrastructure and data.

---

### What I work on

- **Economic analysis and appraisal:** cost–benefit analysis, economic evaluation of infrastructure projects, impact assessment
- **Applied statistics and econometrics:** regression modelling, panel data, time-series forecasting, survey design and sampling
- **Data science:** data cleaning and pipelines, predictive modelling, reproducible analysis
- **Communicating results:** dashboards, policy briefs and visualisations for non-technical decision-makers
